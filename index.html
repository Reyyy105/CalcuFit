Full description

Know your numbers with CalcuFit, a simple BMI calculator and body-weight tracker designed to work offline.

Enter your height, weight, age, and gender to view an estimated BMI, its category, and an estimated healthy-weight range. A clear dashboard brings your latest calculation and profile details together, so you can review your result at a glance.

Features

  • Calculate BMI from your height and weight.
  • See a clear BMI category and visual result indicator.
  • View an estimated healthy-weight range based on BMI.
  • Keep the most recent calculation saved locally on your device.
  • Use the core calculator without an internet connection.

Your measurements and saved result stay in the app’s local storage on your device. CalcuFit does not send them to the developer.

CalcuFit Product Requirement Document (PRD) v1.0 - FUNCTIONAL SPEC

Target Audience: Users, Stakeholders & Mobile Evaluation Board

1. Overview & Value Proposition

CalcuFit: Daily BMI Assessment and Body Weight Tracker is engineered to provide an accessible, lightweight, and completely offline health monitoring experience.

"Know your numbers with CalcuFit, a simple BMI calculator and body-weight tracker designed to work offline. Enter your height, weight, age, and gender to view an estimated BMI, its category, and an estimated healthy-weight range. A clear dashboard brings your latest calculation and profile details together, so you can review your result at a glance."

2. Target User Personas & Use Cases

Fitness Conscious Students

Requires quick daily BMI checks between classes without relying on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Health & Weight Watchers

Needs reliable healthy weight boundaries computed instantly based on individual height and body profile metrics.

3. Core Product Features

  • Offline Functionality: Operates entirely without internet connection or external server requests.
  • Demographic Customization: Input age steppers and gender selections to personalize health assessments.
  • Visual Gauge Dashboard: 360-degree SVG circular progress meter with dynamic color coding corresponding to WHO BMI thresholds.
  • Healthy Weight Bounds: Automatically calculates minimum and maximum healthy weight ranges relative to entered height.
  • Data Privacy Assurance: All records remain locally cached on the user's device storage with zero remote telemetry.

CalcuFit Privacy Policy 100% LOCAL STORAGE

Committed to Zero Data Collection & Complete Offline Privacy

1. Information We Collect & Store

CalcuFit is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. Your measurements (height, weight, age, and gender) and calculated BMI results remain strictly in the app’s local device storage (TinyDB).

2. Zero Internet Transmission

CalcuFit does not transmit your personal health measurements or calculated statistics to the developer, external servers, or third-party analytics providers. The app operates completely offline without network permissions.

3. Data Removal

You can clear your saved calculation history and cached local data at any time by clearing the app’s local storage through your Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.
