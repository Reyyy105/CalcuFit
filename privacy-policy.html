Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the CalcuFit mobile application (“CalcuFit” or “the app”) handles information when you use its BMI calculator and body-weight tracking features.

Information you enter

CalcuFit lets you enter measurements and profile details such as height, weight, age, and gender. It uses these values to calculate and display an estimated body mass index (BMI), a BMI category, and an estimated healthy-weight range. The app stores the most recent calculation on your device using the app’s local storage so it can be available when you return to the app.

Information collection and sharing

CalcuFit does not send the measurements or profile details you enter to the developer. The app does not include an account sign-in, advertising, analytics, or telemetry. The app’s calculator is designed to work offline, and the app does not sell or share your entered information with the developer or other parties.

Your device’s operating system and the app store may separately process information under their own policies when you download, install, or use your device. Those practices are outside this app’s local storage.

Storage and deletion

The most recent calculation is kept in the app’s local storage on your device. It remains there until it is replaced or you clear CalcuFit’s app storage or uninstall the app. You can usually clear app storage from your Android device’s system settings. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove the saved calculation.

Security

Because saved calculation data is kept on your device and is not transmitted by CalcuFit, protect access to your device and use its available screen-lock and security settings. No method of local storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

CalcuFit does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not transmit information entered into the calculator to the developer.

Health information notice

BMI and the related ranges shown by CalcuFit are general informational estimates, not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Individual health needs can differ; consult a healthcare professional for personal guidance.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the CalcuFit app’s Google Play Store listing.
